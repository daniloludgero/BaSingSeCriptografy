# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **BaSingSeCriptografy** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and scientifically rigorous community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* **Rigorous, Blameless Technical Critique**: Critiquing mathematical constructions, S-box properties, MDS matrices, and differential trails with objective evidence (scripts, counterexamples, CNF/SMT witnesses) rather than personal attacks.
* **Intellectual Honesty**: Openly acknowledging failed hypotheses, solver timeouts, and structural weaknesses (as exemplified in `RELATORIO_FINAL.md` v0 -> v4).
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other researchers, students, and developers.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments directed at contributors or researchers.
* Dishonest manipulation of benchmarks, hiding negative cryptanalysis results, or fabricating security claims.
* Public or private harassment, trolling, or publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities & Graduated Sanctions

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Conflict Recusal
If a Code of Conduct report involves a maintainer or reviewer, that individual is strictly recused from all incident deliberations and enforcement decisions.

### Enforcement Ladder

1. **Level 1 — Correction**:
   * **Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or unprofessional tone in a technical review.
   * **Consequence**: A private, written warning from maintainers providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Level 2 — Warning & Temporary Restriction**:
   * **Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions after a Level 1 correction.
   * **Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior, accompanied by a **14-day temporary ban** from repository interactions (Issues, PRs, Discussions).
3. **Level 3 — Permanent Expulsion**:
   * **Impact**: A pattern of violation of community standards, sustained harassment, or deliberate malicious sabotage.
   * **Consequence**: A **permanent ban** from any sort of public interaction within the project spaces and immediate revocation of repository permissions.

---

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project leadership via GitHub's private reporting channels or by contacting the lead maintainer (**Danilo Ludgero**) directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
